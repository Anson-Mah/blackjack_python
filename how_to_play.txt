How to Play: Blackjack

OBJECTIVE:
Win money by creating hands that are higher than the dealer's hand but do not exceed 21.


CARD VALUES:
Cards from 2-10 are worth their face value.
Face cards are worth 10.
Aces are worth 11 or 1, depending on which is more advantageous to the hand.


ROUND START:
You and the dealer are initially dealt two cards each.
The dealer passes out one card face up to you and themself. 
Next, the dealer passes out one more card face up to you, but one card face down for their own hand. 


BLACKJACK:
If your initial two cards make a value of 21, this is called a BLACKJACK.
If the dealer does not also have a BLACKJACK, then you immediately win an amount equal to 1.5 times your initial bet and the hand immediately ends.


PLAYER DECISIONS:

You will always take your turn first before the dealer takes their turn.

 Hit: 
 Take an additional card from the deck and add it to your hand.
 If your hand becomes over 21 after hitting, this is called a BUST and you automatically lose your bet.
 You may hit as many times as you like, as long as you do not bust.

 Stand: 
 End your turn and keep all cards dealt to you.

 Double Down:
 Double your initial bet and add exactly one more card to your hand.

 Split:
 You can decide to split your cards only if the 2 cards originally dealt to you are the same value.
 Splitting turns the pair into two individual hands, each with their own bet equal to the player's initial bet.
 The dealer then deals you two additional cards to complete each hand.

 Surrender:
 Forfeit half your bet and end the hand immediately.


DEALER'S TURN:
Once you have finished playing your hand, the dealer's turn begins.
The dealer begins their turn by revealing their facedown card.
Unlike the player, the dealer has specific rules regarding their play which must be followed at all times.
If the dealer has anything under 17, then the must HIT.
If the dealer has 17 or over, then they must STAND.
The dealer cannot DOUBLE DOWN nor SPLIT.
Once the dealer finishes playing their hand, you compare your hand against the dealer's.

If the dealer's hand is closer to 21 than yours, you lose your entire bet. 
If your hand is closer to 21 than the dealer's, you win an amount equal to your bet. 
If the dealer busts, you win an amount equal to your bet. 
If you and your dealer have the same value, then you PUSH (Tie), and no exchange of bets is made.
